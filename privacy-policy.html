Chroma Ray Privacy Policy

English 한국어

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Chroma Ray (the "App") is a puzzle game about bending and mixing light with mirrors, developed by J.Biglion. This policy explains how the App handles information.

Summary
  • There is no sign-up, and the App does not collect your name, email address or phone number.
  • Game progress and settings are stored on your device. The developer has no server that receives them.
  • The App shows Google AdMob ads. Google may collect information such as your advertising ID for this.
  • There are no in-app purchases.

1. Information stored on your device

The information below stays in the App's storage on your device. It is not sent to the developer, and the developer cannot access it.

InformationPurpose
Stars and best records per stage, remaining hintsKeeping your game progress
Whether the tutorial was seen, devices already introducedNot repeating guides
Total clear count, when the next full-screen ad is dueSpacing full-screen ads
Settings (sound, vibration, grid, color letters, high-contrast lights, language)Keeping your preferences

Reset progress in Settings deletes stage records and stars. Settings, remaining hints, tutorial status and the clear count remain. Uninstalling the App removes all of this from the App's storage on your device.

Android backup and device transfer: The App allows Android system backup. Depending on your device and account settings, this information may be included in cloud backups or transfers to a new device. Those backups are managed by the operating system or device provider, not the developer, and may remain after you uninstall the App.

The App does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows banner ads on the home and clear screens, and a full-screen ad after the 20th clear and after every 10th clear from then on. When you are out of hints, you may choose to watch a rewarded ad. Ad content is limited to the G rating. Google and its partners may collect and use the following for ad delivery and measurement:

  • Advertising ID (Android advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Ad impressions and clicks

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Menu names vary by device.

Retention follows Google's policies. Even with personalized ads turned off, information needed to deliver and measure non-personalized ads may still be sent.

5. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent regarding ads through the Google User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time under Settings > Privacy options in the App.

6. Retention and deletion

The developer does not store personal information on any server. Information stored by the App is removed from the App's storage on that device when you uninstall the App. Existing Android backups may remain separately or be restored when you reinstall.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, or ask that its processing be stopped. Because the developer holds no personal information, requests about information collected by Google can be made through Google's own tools and guidance.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its effective date.
