Light adds up

Unlike paint, light gets brighter as it mixes. Turn the lights on and see what the crystal needs.

A crystal lights only when the color arriving is exactly its own.

Easy to pick up

Every control is a tap or a drag. The first stages show you the rest.

1

Place

Tap an empty cell to place a piece from your inventory.

2

Rotate and move

Tap a piece to rotate it. Drag it to move it, or drag it off the board to take it back.

3

Light every crystal

Clear with no hint and few moves to earn all three stars.

A new device keeps it fresh

Devices arrive one at a time, each with a short guide the first time you meet it.

Filter

Lets only its own color through.

Splitter

Sends light both straight ahead and to the side.

Prism

Splits light: red turns left, green goes straight, blue turns right.

Portal

Light leaves the portal with the same number, going the same way.

Inverter

Changes light to its opposite color. Red becomes cyan.

One-sided mirror

The bright side reflects. The dotted side blocks.

Combiner

Merges incoming light and sends it where the arrow points.

Switch door

Blocks light until the crystal with the same number lights up.

Forbidden crystal

No light may touch it. Send the beam elsewhere.

See it in play

Made to be comfortable

Color-blind friendly options

Color letters are on by default, and high-contrast lights widen the color spacing.

Hints when you are stuck

You start with 3 hints. Watch an optional video ad for one more when you run out.

Ads, plainly

Banners appear on the home and clear screens, and a full-screen ad after every 10th clear starting from the 20th. The play screen has no banner. No in-app purchases.
